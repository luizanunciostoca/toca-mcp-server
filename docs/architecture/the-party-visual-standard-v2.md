## Standards

The stable standard IDs are preserved while their canonical visual version advances to `2.0`:

- `THE_PARTY_HYBRID_NETWORKS_V1`
- `THE_PARTY_HYBRID_MINIMALIST_V1`

The stable IDs avoid breaking downstream contracts; `standardVersion=2.0` is the version discriminator used by the Drive content registry and runtime orchestration.

## Palette

The canonical digital palette is:

- black `#0D0D0D`
- white `#FFFFFF`
- gold `#FFC629`
- amber `#FFA900`
- neon purple `#8A2BE2`
- red `#FF2D20`

Purple/violet is the primary controlled energy for `INTERNATIONAL`. Gold, amber, orange and warm red are the controlled energy for `NATIONAL`. These are visual constraints, not permission to recolor real people or venue truth globally.

## Typography

- Display: `Bebas Neue Condensed Bold`
- Expressive emphasis: `Brush Script`
- Support/body: `Montserrat Regular / Medium / Bold`

Font naming in the standard is a design contract. Production rendering still requires a licensed and pinned font source before typography can be treated as byte-deterministic.

## Brand and footer

The hero brand is `BRAND-THE-PARTY-WHITE-V1`.

When the institutional footer applies, its order is exact:

`TOCA_DO_MORCEGO -> CORONA -> RED_BULL -> MORRO_DIGITAL`

Only registered official brand assets may be used. AI reconstruction, typographic approximation or redrawing of logos is forbidden.

## Hybrid Networks

Used for high-impact campaigns, line-up, events, activations, promotions and immersive social content.

`INTERNATIONAL` or `NATIONAL` is mandatory. The runtime must never infer environment from artist, image, color, prior event or audience origin. Missing environment remains fail-closed.

The v2.0 layout vocabulary includes two-track dual experience, operational hours/attributes, drinks/bar CTA, Sunset-to-Party continuity, DJ/high-impact and line-up compositions.

## Hybrid Minimalist

Used for institutional communication, elegant ads, invitations, highlights, landing pages, websites and people-first conversion.

The system prioritizes real people, real venue photography, lower graphic density, greater negative space, one primary message and one primary CTA.

## Content registry contract

`CONTENT_ITEMS.creative_standard_version` for The Party canonical records is `2.0`.

`GoogleSheetsThePartyContentOrchestration` rejects version drift with `THE_PARTY_CONTENT_STANDARD_VERSION_MISMATCH`. The standard ID must still match the approved intent. Hybrid Networks still requires explicit environment before it can resolve beyond `BLOCKED_NEEDS_ENVIRONMENT`.

## Quality and publication boundary

The v2.0 mirror requires:

- operation-scoped venue reference set;
- exact canonical Drive bindings;
- palette and typography contract;
- official hero brand and footer order;
- visual regression against v2.0 references;
- Brand Integrity PASS;
- Venue Fidelity PASS;
- Quality PASS;
- exact output SHA-256 binding before publication.

This synchronization does not authorize provider writes or production deployment. Approval, policy, scheduler and provider permissions remain independent gates.

## Known external-source blockers

The following remain intentionally unresolved until source artifacts are supplied and verified:

- licensed/pinned font files;
- vector/source masters where not already registered;
- favicon/source icon package when required;
- formally approved clear-space and minimum-size measurements.

These must not be invented from flattened reference images.
