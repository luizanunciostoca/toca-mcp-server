# The Party creative standard

Status: active canonical integration contract on the Creative Truth branch.

## Purpose

Make the official **The Party — Sistema Híbrido + Minimalista** identity mandatory for every final creative with `operation=THE_PARTY`, while preserving the parent `TOCA_CREATIVE_TRUTH_POLICY_V1` boundaries.

The visual source of truth is Google Drive document `THE_PARTY — 02_IDENTIDADE_VISUAL_DO_PRODUTO` (`1yFY-1NXjWs1bKvRP3smRuRKWT6OR3WK-FkDcoLqAmPk`), including its canonical v1.2 addendum. The user-supplied official visual reference is Drive file `1-xLSxr4qlKg-3OrI5hTPUpVvsAgqi2gD`. The official The Party hero logo is Drive file `1V09F8w1BcgwzONnZk1ROpOJACuDF2dPF` and is registered with a pinned SHA-256.

Repository mirrors exist only to make this contract deterministic, testable and auditable. Drive remains the business/visual source of truth.

## Mandatory standards

Every final creative with `operation=THE_PARTY` must resolve exactly one The Party-specific standard before composition:

- `THE_PARTY_HYBRID_NETWORKS_V1` — Family 01, **Identidade Híbrida nas Redes**;
- `THE_PARTY_HYBRID_MINIMALIST_V1` — Family 02, **Identidade Visual Híbrida Minimalista**.

A generic `ALL` standard may not replace a The Party-specific visual standard for a final The Party creative. Generic video/thumbnail standards may still provide cross-cutting transport/export constraints, but the visual identity must remain bound to one of the two The Party standards above.

## Family 01 — Hybrid Networks

Use for high-impact campaigns, line-ups, events, activations, social promotion and pieces that need stronger immersion.

The family keeps one brand but permits two controlled energy directions:

- **International environment:** purple/violet energy;
- **National environment:** gold/amber/orange/warm-red energy.

These colors are accents or real-light cues, not permission for destructive global filters. Real skin, real materials and recognizable Toca venue elements must remain believable.

The composition can be denser than the minimalist family, but it still needs a dominant idea, a clear hierarchy and one primary CTA.

## Family 02 — Hybrid Minimalist

Use for institutional communication, elegant ads, invitations, highlight covers, landing pages, websites and people/experience-first conversion.

The visual base is dark/night, warm white and gold/amber. Purple and red are restrained accents or real-light cues. The composition uses more negative space, fewer graphic elements and lets the audience, artist or lived experience become the protagonist.

## Brand architecture

`THE_PARTY` is the hero brand and must be materialized only from the official registered asset. It is never typed, approximated or generated by AI.

When institutional/partner footer is applicable, the canonical order is:

`TOCA_DO_MORCEGO` → `CORONA` → `RED_BULL` → `MORRO_DIGITAL`.

All marks must come from the Brand Asset Registry. AI reconstruction, redrawing, distortion or logo substitution is a hard failure.

## Venue truth and photography

Real Toca photography is the default visual truth. Prioritize people, crowd, DJs, drinks, real lighting, architecture and recognizable experience. Generic nightclub imagery is not an acceptable substitute.

The system must not invent a stage, bar, terrace, architecture, furniture, view, circulation path or fixed decoration. A static-image `GENERATIVE_EXCEPTION` remains possible only under the parent Creative Truth policy: explicit approval, verified reference set, byte-bound references, post-generation review, Venue Fidelity, Brand Integrity and Quality gates.

Full synthetic video generation remains unsupported in V1.

## Multi-format application

- Story: 1080×1920 / 9:16, protected safe area, quick hierarchy, hero mark and clear CTA.
- Feed: 1080×1350 / 4:5 preferred; 1:1 only when needed.
- Ads: 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16 by placement; critical message, CTA and signature must survive crops.
- Reel/video: real approved Toca shots; motion inherits the selected family without distorting marks or inventing space.
- Thumbnail/cover: inherits the selected The Party family and remains under Creative Truth exact-output binding.

## Runtime enforcement

The Creative Truth resolver must reject `operation=THE_PARTY` when the resolved `standardId` is not The Party-specific. This prevents `TOCA_VIDEO_V1`, `TOCA_THUMBNAIL_V1` or another transversal standard from silently becoming the final visual identity.

The deterministic image composer applies The Party family-specific layout treatment while preserving real venue bytes, official asset insertion, lineage and exact output hashing. The Party hero logo is separated from the institutional footer.

## Publication boundary

Visual compliance does not authorize publication. Approval, Policy Engine, provider permissions and exact-asset publication binding remain unchanged. Publication must consume the exact approved final bytes and must not rebuild the creative.
